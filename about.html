Who We Are

About Prodigy Society

A student-led innovation and technology club dedicated to leadership, creativity, public speaking, technology, design, teamwork, hackathons, community service, and personal development.

Our Story

Born from Ambition

Prodigy Society was founded in 2083 (BS) at Tilottama Campus by a group of passionate students who believed that leadership and innovation should be accessible to every student—not just a select few.

Based in Tilottama Yogikuti, Rupandehi, Nepal, we operate as an independent student body focused on practical skill-building, collaborative projects, and community impact.

Today we run workshops, host hackathons, organize leadership programs, and partner with local organizations to create opportunities that prepare students for the real world.
